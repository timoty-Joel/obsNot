# Text Data
⇒ Unstructured or Semi-unstructured data basically composed of text such as reviews, articles, social media posts, and text documents.  
⇒ Can capture humans reviews, sentiments, opinions or experiences, providing insights to certain goal. 

**Challenge in Handling Text Data**  
Working with text data comes with its own obstacle such as:
- **Unstructured Nature**: Text data lacks a predefined structure, existing in free-flowing format that can be challenging for for computers to analyze. Unlike data in spreadsheet or table that has column and row, text data can be a paragraph, a document, a tweet, an instagram post, etc. 
- **Variability in Length and Style**: Documents can range from shorts tweets or reviews to long articles, need normalization for analysis purposes. 
- **Ambiguity & Subjectivity**: Words in text data can have multiple meanings and interpretations depending on the context. For instance, word "happy" can express positive emotions or describe a state of good fortune. 
- **Noise & Inconsistencies**: Text can **contain typos, abbrevations, slang, and emojis** that make nomalization and cleaning are necessary. Texts written in all capslock and excessive use of exclamation points
